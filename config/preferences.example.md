# My inbox preferences

Copy to config/preferences.md (git-ignored). The manager agent reads it on every run to decide what
makes your digest. Write in plain language and be specific; the more concrete, the better the filtering.

## About me
- Role / job, team, what you're working on right now.
- Projects, courses, or clients whose emails always matter.

## Always show me (act on)
- Anything that asks me personally to reply, approve, sign up, or submit something.
- Emails from my manager, direct reports, or these people: ...
- Deadlines, meeting changes, and anything time-sensitive about my projects.

## Interested (worth a look)
- Industry news about: ...
- Talks, events, or opportunities about: ...

## Not interested (leave out)
- Marketing, promotions, sales, and sponsored newsletter sections.
- Automated notifications (CI, ticket updates) unless they mention me.
- Repeated reminder emails for events I'm not attending.
